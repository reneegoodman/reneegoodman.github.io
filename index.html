Hi, I'm Renee Goodman

Physics • Neuroscience • Intelligence

Welcome. I am a PhD student at the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization, located in Göttingen, Germany. I use tools from statistical physics and network theory to analyze large connectomics datasets, such as the Female Adult Fly Brain to understand how brain networks are organized, and how they process information.
